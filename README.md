# Sweeet Network Site

Create the complete redesign of Sweeet Network (sweeetnetwork.com) as a vibrant, bright, community-led food and pop-culture lifestyle media platform based on the attached business plan and HEADERS content doc.

Design & Style Inspiration:
- Look and feel inspired by https://isaidwhatisaidpodcast.com/ (ISWIS): bold rounded cards, chunky borders, playful drop-shadows, sticker badges, retro-chic pills, punchy uppercase headings with playful font pairing, vibrant tickers, and engaging interactive widgets.
- Color palette: bright, energetic, confectionery-inspired candy colors — electric bubblegum pink, warm saffron/buttercup yellow, vivid lilac/purple, juicy tangerine orange, mint accent, and deep rich plum/blackberry for typography and high-contrast borders.
- Tone: fun, relatable Nigerian millennial/Gen-Z culture meets culinary love ("Sweeeties", "A little culture. A little chaos. A lot of Sweeet").

Site Structure & Pages (All 7 Headers from HEADERS.md):
1. Home:
   - "Sugar, Spice, Everything Nice!" hero with bold animated ticker, featured video/stories slider, and quick-access categories.
   - Social highlights (YouTube thumbnails, Instagram/TikTok previews).
   - "A Little Sweeet in Your Inbox" newsletter card.
   - "Love the stuff we do at Sweeet? Partner with us today" collaboration banner.
   - Quick preview highlights from The Digest, Community, and Playground.

2. The Digest (Editorial & Magazine):
   - First Slice (First impressions):
     * Ayra Starr’s "Starrgirl" Album (purple aesthetic, confident-girl era, full article text + First Slice verdict).
     * Grammy Season 2027 & Nigerian Artists submissions (Davido, Tems, Asake, Ayra Starr, NO11 debate).
     * Spotify Audiobooks in Nigeria (₦2,300 monthly, discussion poll: does listening to an audiobook count as reading?).
   - Trending Stories:
     * Ayra Starr First African Artist to Headline NFL International Halftime Show at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.
     * Chimeka Garricks announces new novel "A Prayer for Wolves" (Masobe Books).
     * Rachel Ojuromi (@giverachelashot) from Lagos street style to walking for CHANEL in Paris.
   - Interactive article reader views, categories filter, search bar, and comment/reaction counters.

3. Sweeet Box (Social Hub):
   - Curated feed of Sweeet Network's latest YouTube videos, Instagram reels, and viral social clips with playback/preview modals, view counts, and direct links to YouTube and Instagram.

4. Community:
   - "Pass the Pepper": Hot takes & debate board (financial responsibility in your 20s, Nigerian parenting, beauty standards, marriage vs wedding culture) with agree/disagree/reaction buttons.
   - "Sweet or Sour?": Interactive swipe or vote cards (e.g., Tele X Zikora, Pineapple on pizza) with instant percentage reveals.
   - "Question of the Day": Weekly prompt ("What’s the one snack you’d never ever share?") with answer input form and live response counter badge ("127 Sweeeties have answered").
   - "Sweeet Spot": Handpicked verified Lagos restaurants with photo galleries, ambience notes, menus, and signature dish recommendations.
   - "Sweeet’s Choice": Certified food & dessert vendors (Butterland, Crumb & Co., Scoop Society, Ember Grill House, Blush Desserts) + "Become a Certified Vendor" application form.
   - "Spill the Tea": Anonymous confession & gossip box ("Funny moments, crazy experiences, random gist — spill the tea!").

5. Playground (Interactive Games & Arcade):
   - Conversation Card Games with deck selector and card flip animation:
     * "Conversation Starters for Friends" (all 20 prompts from doc)
     * "First Date Questions" (all 20 prompts from doc)
     * "Deep Questions" (all 20 prompts from doc)
   - "Accept or Decline Cookies?": Playable dilemma game featuring the Nigerian internet search prompts (e.g., "Will making eba by 12 AM make me fat?", "How to lose 50 pounds but still eat pounded yam?", "I told them I'm 5 minutes away but still at home").
   - Playable Tic-Tac-Toe (Cupcakes vs Donuts / Sweeet icons).
   - "This or That" quick-fire game.
   - General Leaderboard & Teasers for Would You Rather, Imposter Game, and Snack Personality Test.

6. Shop (Sweeet Merch):
   - Limited-edition Sweeet merch collection: Graphic tees ("Sugar, Spice & Everything Nice"), tote bags, dad caps, foodie sticker packs, and enamel pins with shopping cart simulation.

7. About:
   - Sweeet Network story, Executive Summary, Mission & Vision ("food as memory, culture, and connection"), Brand Values (Creativity, Authenticity, Community, Innovation & Excellence), and company credentials (RC Number: 8938673, Base: Lagos, Nigeria).

Include a sticky header with the Sweeet logo, navigation links to all 7 sections, search modal, and newsletter CTA, plus a comprehensive footer with links, email signup, and social handles.
